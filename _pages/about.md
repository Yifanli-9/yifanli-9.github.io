Welcome! I am a researcher working on climate science and hydrological modeling.

My research interests include climate projection, land surface and river modeling, and the application of machine learning to Earth system science.

Honors & Awards
======
- **Matsuno Award (松野賞)**, Meteorological Society of Japan, 2024 — awarded for the presentation "Evaluation of the Isotope-enabled Fully Coupled Model MIROC6-iso" at the MSJ 2024 Autumn Meeting ([awardees list](https://www.metsoc.jp/about/awards/matsuno))

Editing this site
======
- **This page**: edit `_pages/about.md`
- **Sidebar info (name, affiliation, links)**: edit the `author:` section in `_config.yml`
- **Profile photo**: replace `images/profile.png` with your own photo (keep the same filename)
- **Publications**: add one Markdown file per paper in `_publications/` (the existing files are examples to copy from)
- **Talks / Teaching / CV**: same idea — see `_talks/`, `_teaching/`, `_pages/cv.md`

Every push to the `main` branch automatically rebuilds the site within a minute or two.
